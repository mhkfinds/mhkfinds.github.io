Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

What we collect

If you give us your email (to hear about deals) or submit a deal through our form, we collect exactly what you enter — nothing more. We also use Google Analytics to see general site usage, like which pages get visited — this doesn't identify you personally.

Why we collect it

Email addresses are used to let you know about deals and updates from MHKfinds. Form submissions (deals, business info) are used to review and potentially list deals on the site. That's it.

We don't sell your info

Your email and anything you submit is never sold or shared with third parties. It's used by MHKfinds only.

Opting out

You can ask to be removed from our email list any time by messaging @mhkfinds on Instagram or emailing mhkfinds@gmail.com. Once we start sending regular deal emails, every one will include an easy one-click unsubscribe link too.

Questions?

DM @mhkfinds on Instagram or email mhkfinds@gmail.com.
